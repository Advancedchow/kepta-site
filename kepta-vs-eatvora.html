Kepta vs Eatvora

Kepta and Eatvora both help you track the food in your kitchen and waste less of it. They make different choices about what's free and what's paid. This page lays out the differences plainly, so you can pick the one that fits your kitchen.

Everything on this page about Eatvora was checked against eatvora.app and their US App Store listing in September 2026. Their pricing and plans can change. If something here is out of date, tell us at support@kepta.kitchen and we'll fix it. Eatvora is a trademark of its owner. Kepta isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Eatvora, and we're just comparing.

The short version

Eatvora in short

A household-management app with a free plan and two paid tiers. As of September 2026, features like AI suggestions, unlimited items, family sharing, and monthly reports sit in the paid tiers.

Kepta in short

A smaller, focused app that launches free: full inventory, family sharing, and honest money numbers, with no item caps, no ads, and no tracking.

Eatvora ships several things Kepta doesn't have at all yet, and we say so below.

Where Eatvora is ahead today

Honestly, in a few places. As of September 2026, Eatvora ships all of these:

  • ●Barcode scanning
  • ●Receipt scanning
  • ●AI recipe and autopilot suggestions
  • ●Smart shopping tips
  • ●Meal planning
  • ●Chore tracking with AI chore planning
  • ●Calendar integration
  • ●Monthly and PDF reports

Kepta has none of those today. If scanning items in by barcode or having AI plan your week is what you're after, Eatvora is the stronger pick right now.

What Kepta does instead

Kepta puts its effort into the everyday loop: knowing what's in your kitchen, what to use next, and what it's really costing you.

  • A freshness color for every item, based on that food's own shelf life, so you always know what to use next
  • A daily reminder that names the actual food about to turn, not a generic ping
  • A shopping list with aisle grouping that moves straight into your kitchen, prices included
  • Family sharing, offline support, and one-tap undo everywhere, all free
  • No ads and no tracking: your email and your food data are used to run the app, nothing else
  • Delete your account any time, right inside the app

Which one should you pick?

Pick Eatvora if…

You want barcode and receipt scanning, AI-generated suggestions, meal and chore planning, or calendar integration today. You're comfortable with a subscription for household sharing and unlimited items.

Wait for Kepta if…

You want your whole family in one kitchen for free, no cap on how much food you track, and money numbers that only ever count real dollars. You can live without scanning and AI for now.
