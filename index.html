Kepta

Waste less, save money.

Kepta keeps track of the food in your fridge, freezer, and pantry, warns you before it goes bad, and shows what your food waste really costs.

●Every item gets a freshness color based on its own shelf life, so you always know what to use next.
●Recipes are ranked by what you already have, with the food that is about to turn pushed to the top.
●Real money only. Kepta counts what you actually spent, so your wasted and saved numbers stay honest.
