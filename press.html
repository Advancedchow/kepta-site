About Kepta

Kepta is an iPhone app that keeps track of the food in your fridge, freezer, and pantry. It warns you before food goes bad, helps you cook what you already have, and shows what your waste really costs. It is built for people and families who want to throw away less food and spend less at the store.

Kepta is made by Effort Zero, LLC in Ohio.

Key Features

Add in Seconds

Tap a picture tile for staples like milk or eggs, or type a couple of letters and pick from the suggestions. Kepta guesses the category, the spot, and the shelf life for you.

Colors Tell You What to Use

Every item gets a ring and a color from how much of its life is left, with dates that fit the food itself and where you keep it. Raw meat and seafood in the fridge count down to cook instead, and fridge leftovers count down to eat, so you know what to use first.

  • Fresh
  • Soon
  • Past

Cook What You Have

More than 100 recipes, ranked by what's actually in your kitchen. Dishes you can make right now come first, and anything that rescues expiring food gets a boost. Each has step by step directions with real amounts and a Watch on YouTube button, and you can save your own.

See Honest Money

When food gets used, you saved what it cost. When it gets tossed, you wasted it. Kepta adds up the prices you enter, and if you skip one it uses a plain typical price you can change. No AI ever guesses a price.

One Kitchen for the Whole Family

Your kitchen has a six-character invite code. Share it, and family members join your kitchen from their own phones. Everyone sees the same shelves, the same list, the same leftovers.

Reminders and a Shopping List

  • Daily reminders that name real items ("use the chicken"), not generic nags
  • Shopping list grouped by aisle, flowing straight into your kitchen with prices

What Makes It Different

  • No item limits. The free version tracks as much food as you own.
  • Free family sharing. Sharing a kitchen needs no paid plan.
  • Money at your own prices. Only the part of a food that gets tossed counts as wasted, and homemade meals count as dishes, never dollars.
  • A date for each food, with a freshness ring. Strawberries get a few days and crackers get months, not one date for a whole category.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your email and kitchen data are used only to run Kepta.
