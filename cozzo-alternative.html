A new home after CozZo

CozZo helped a lot of households run a waste-free kitchen, and it deserved a better ending. If you kept your fridge in it and now have nowhere to put your kitchen, this page is an honest look at whether Kepta fits, including where it doesn't yet.

Kepta is in App Store review right now. Want to know the day it lands? Ask for a launch email.

What happened to CozZo

By its makers' own account, the service ended because the cloud infrastructure that supported it was being shut down, not because they chose to walk away from it. They responded generously: a farewell version with every premium feature unlocked, supported through December 30, 2025. As of September 2026, the app is no longer available on the US App Store.

Their goodbye note was warm to the end.

"Thank you for every moment you've spent with CozZo."The CozZo team, in their farewell announcement

Where Kepta stands

Kepta is a kitchen inventory and food-waste app for iPhone. It is in App Store review right now, so you can't download it yet. Everything in the CozZo column below comes from CozZo's own website, and every comparison is accurate as of September 2026.

CozZo is a trademark of its owner. Kepta isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the CozZo team, and this page isn't a review of their work: it's a plain accounting of what carries over and what doesn't. If something here is wrong, tell us at support@kepta.kitchen and we'll fix it.

Feature by feature

Your food inventory Covered

CozZo had

One inventory from fridge to pantry, tracking "Best By" and "Use By" dates for all foods.

Kepta

Fridge, freezer, and pantry, plus custom places you name yourself. Instead of typed package dates, every item gets a freshness color from a USDA-researched shelf-life database, relative to that food's own shelf life.

Household sharing Covered

CozZo had

Up to 10 household members with real-time sync of products, inventory, and shopping lists, part of the paid Household Account.

Kepta

Family sharing is free. One 6-character invite code, like ABC-123, puts your household in the same kitchen. Items, the list, and the money numbers stay in sync for everyone.

Expiry warnings Different

CozZo had

"Best By" and "Use By" tracking that helped you decide whether food had to be binned.

Kepta

A daily reminder that names the actual items to use soon, plus freshness colors on every row. You never type dates: Kepta estimates from what the food is and where it lives. It's quality guidance, not safety advice, and it says so.

Recipes from what you have Covered

CozZo had

AI matching of recipe ingredients to the products in your stock, part of the Household Account.

Kepta

A Cook tab of roughly 80 recipes ranked by what's already in your kitchen, with expiring food boosted to the top and YouTube videos to cook along. Free.

Shopping lists Different

CozZo had

Three lists free; the subscription unlocked unlimited lists.

Kepta

One shopping list, grouped by aisle, that converts straight into your kitchen with prices as you buy. Nothing on it is capped, but it is one list, not several.

Leftovers Covered

CozZo had

Leftovers listed in the meal planner and tracked for freshness.

Kepta

Log leftovers in a tap and watch their freshness like any other item. Homemade meals are counted as dishes, never fake dollar estimates, and restaurant leftovers keep their real cost.

Barcode scanning Not Yet

CozZo had

A scanner that recognized over half a billion products, including package sizes and photos.

Kepta

Not yet. Adding food is fast type-ahead with smart guesses for category, place, and price, but there is no scanner in the launch version. Barcode scanning comes later.

Receipt scanning Not Yet

CozZo had

A receipt reader that pulled purchased items from up to 5 receipts at once.

Kepta

Not yet. There is no receipt reader in the launch version.

Meal planning Not Yet

CozZo had

A versatile weekly planner integrated with its recipes and food inventories.

Kepta

Not yet. The Cook tab tells you what to make today from what you have, but there's no calendar planning.

Beyond the kitchen Different

CozZo had

Seven extra space types: cupboards, storage room, medicine box, bathroom.

Kepta

Kepta is food only, on purpose. Custom places are all kinds of fridge, freezer, or pantry, because the freshness engine is built for food. If you tracked your medicine box in CozZo, Kepta won't replace that.

What Kepta adds

  • ●Honest money tracking: the prices you enter, charged only for what you waste, with no AI guessing and no fake dollars for home cooking.
  • ●A daily reminder that names real food, the actual items to use soon, not a generic ping.
  • ●One-tap undo everywhere, so a mis-tap never costs you an item or a log entry.
  • ●Offline support: add food and browse your kitchen without a connection.
  • ●No item caps, no ads, no tracking. Privacy is just your email and your own content, used only to run the app.
  • ●Account deletion lives inside the app and removes your data.

Why free matters more after a shutdown

CozZo's makers said the ending was out of their hands: the infrastructure it ran on was shut down. But if you subscribed to the Household Account, the service you were paying for is gone now, and that stings. No app can honestly promise you forever, and Kepta won't pretend to.

What it can tell you plainly: everything on this page ships free. The inventory, the sharing, the recipes, the reminders, and the money numbers are all uncapped, with no ads and no tracking. You're not being asked to bet a subscription on a stranger's app while you're still deciding whether to trust one again.

The name, since you're new here

Kepta comes from kept: a well kept kitchen, money you keep, recipes worth keeping. It's also the Lithuanian word for baked.

The short verdict: Kepta covers the heart of what CozZo did, free, and is plain about what it doesn't do yet.
