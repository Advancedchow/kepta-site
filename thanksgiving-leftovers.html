Thanksgiving Leftovers: How Long They Last and What to Freeze

Most leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator. Get them there within 2 hours of cooking, and reheat them to 165°F before you eat them.

3 to 4 days

2 hours

Store it, freeze it, heat it up

Bacteria grow rapidly between 40°F and 140°F, so the goal is to move food through that range quickly, on the way down and on the way back up.

Cooling and storing

  • Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if it is above 90°F.
  • Throw away perishable food that sat at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
  • Divide large amounts into shallow containers so they cool quickly.
  • Cut the turkey into smaller pieces. Slice the breast meat; the legs and wings may be left whole.
  • Hot food can go straight into the refrigerator, or into an ice or cold water bath first.
  • Cover leftovers, wrap them airtight, or seal them in containers.

Freezing and thawing

  • Leftovers keep 3 to 4 months in the freezer under USDA's general guidance; the table above has times for specific dishes.
  • In the refrigerator: use within 3 to 4 days of thawing, or refreeze them.
  • In cold water, sealed in a leak-proof bag: cook before refreezing.
  • In the microwave: heat to 165°F, and the food can then be refrozen.
  • Frozen leftovers can be reheated without thawing first.
  • From a large container thawed in the refrigerator, you can take out a portion and refreeze the rest without reheating it.

Reheating

  • Reheat leftovers to 165°F, measured with a food thermometer.
  • Bring sauces, soups, and gravies to a rolling boil.
  • Cover leftovers while they reheat.
  • In the microwave: cover, rotate, add liquid if needed, and vent the cover.
  • Check the temperature in several places, because microwaves have cold spots, then let the food rest.
  • Anything left after reheating to 165°F is safe to refreeze.

A calm plan to throw less away

Three small habits, most of them done the night of the meal.

  1. Portion it that night

    Before the dishes are done, divide everything into shallow containers and get them into the refrigerator inside the 2-hour window.

  2. Freeze by day 2

    Be honest about what your household will eat. Whatever you won't finish in the next day or two goes into the freezer while it still has days left in the refrigerator.

  3. Label with the date

    Write what it is and the day it was cooked on every container, so nobody has to guess on day four, and nothing gets lost at the back of the freezer.

Quick questions

How long do Thanksgiving leftovers last in the refrigerator?

USDA guidance is 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator for leftovers, including cooked turkey. A few items differ on FoodSafety.gov's Cold Food Storage Chart: quiche keeps 3 to 5 days, cooked store-wrapped ham slices 3 to 5 days, a whole cooked store-wrapped ham and hard-cooked eggs 1 week, and homemade eggnog 2 to 4 days.

How long can Thanksgiving food sit out?

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F. USDA says to throw away perishable food left at room temperature for more than 2 hours, because bacteria grow rapidly between 40°F and 140°F.

Can you freeze leftover turkey?

Yes. FoodSafety.gov lists cooked meat or poultry leftovers at 2 to 6 months in the freezer. USDA notes that frozen leftovers are safe indefinitely but lose moisture and flavor over longer times. Cut the turkey into smaller pieces before you refrigerate or freeze it: slice the breast meat, and the legs and wings may be left whole.

What temperature should leftovers be reheated to?

Reheat leftovers to 165°F, measured with a food thermometer. Bring sauces, soups, and gravies to a rolling boil. In the microwave, cover the food, rotate it, and check the temperature in several places, because microwaves have cold spots.

Can you refreeze leftovers after thawing them?

It depends on how they were thawed. Leftovers thawed in the refrigerator can be refrozen, or used within 3 to 4 days. Leftovers thawed in cold water should be cooked before refreezing, and leftovers thawed in the microwave should be heated to 165°F, then can be refrozen. Food remaining after reheating to 165°F is safe to refreeze.
