Thanksgiving Leftovers: How Long They Last and What to Freeze

Most Thanksgiving leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator. Get them there within 2 hours of cooking, and reheat them to 165°F before eating.

Every food safety number on this page comes from the USDA and FoodSafety.gov, linked at the end of the page.

3 to 4 days

How long leftovers keep in the refrigerator, cooked turkey included.

2 hours

The longest perishable food should sit out. Make it 1 hour if the room is above 90°F.

165°F

The temperature to reheat leftovers to, checked with a food thermometer.

Cooling and storing

Bacteria grow rapidly between 40°F and 140°F, so the goal is to get food cold quickly. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or 1 hour if it's above 90°F. Perishable food left out longer than 2 hours should be thrown away.

  • ●Divide large amounts into shallow containers so they cool fast.
  • ●Cut the turkey into smaller pieces and slice the breast meat. Legs and wings can stay whole.
  • ●Hot food can go straight into the refrigerator, or into an ice or cold water bath first.
  • ●Cover leftovers, wrap them airtight, or seal them in containers.

Freezing and thawing

Leftovers keep 3 to 4 months frozen for best quality. When you want them back, there are three safe ways to thaw:

  • ●In the refrigerator. Use within 3 to 4 days, or refreeze. From a large container thawed this way, you can take a portion and refreeze the rest without reheating.
  • ●In cold water, in a leak-proof bag. Cook it before refreezing.
  • ●In the microwave. Heat to 165°F, and then it can be refrozen.

You can also reheat frozen leftovers without thawing them first.

Reheating

Reheat leftovers to 165°F, measured with a food thermometer. Bring sauces, soups, and gravies to a rolling boil, and cover leftovers while they reheat.

  • ●In the microwave, cover the dish, rotate it, and add a little liquid if it needs it.
  • ●Vent the cover, then check the temperature in several places, because microwaves leave cold spots.
  • ●Let it rest before serving.
  • ●Food left over after reheating to 165°F is safe to refreeze.

A calm plan to throw less away

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. The 3 to 4 day window starts when the meal is cooked, so here is how the weekend can go.

Thursday night

Before the kitchen closes, portion everything into shallow containers and label each one with the date. Anything that sat out more than 2 hours goes, without guilt.

By Saturday, day 2

Be honest about what you'll actually eat. Freeze the rest now, while it still has its best texture, but keep the custard pie and the salads out of the freezer: they don't freeze well.

Sunday and Monday

Days 3 and 4. Finish what's left in the fridge or toss it. A whole store-wrapped ham and hard-cooked eggs get the full week; almost nothing else does.
