How Long Does Bagged Salad Last?

Bagged salad greens keep 3 to 5 days in the refrigerator. USDA's FoodKeeper counts that from the date on the bag for an unopened bag, and gives an opened bag 2 days.

Every storage time on this page comes from the USDA's FoodKeeper data. Last checked November 25, 2026.

Refrigerator, freezer, and pantry times

Freezer times are for best quality. The USDA says frozen foods remain safe indefinitely.

"Not listed" means FoodKeeper gives no time for that spot. "From the date of purchase" and "after opening" are FoodKeeper's own terms.

Can you freeze bagged salad?

FoodKeeper marks freezing as not recommended for bagged greens and for lettuce.

What FoodKeeper adds

  • ●Bagged greens: leaf, spinach, lettuce. Unopened product will last 3 to 5 days after date on bag; after opening it will last 2 days.

Three things worth knowing

Count from the date on the bag

For bagged greens, FoodKeeper counts an unopened bag from the date printed on it, and an opened bag from the day you open it.

Source: FoodKeeper data, USDA

Use the crisper drawer

Sealed crisper drawers provide an optimal storage environment for fruits and vegetables. Vegetables need higher humidity than fruits.

Source: Refrigeration and Food Safety, USDA FSIS

Lettuce doesn't freeze well

USDA names lettuce as an example of a food that simply doesn't freeze well.

Source: Freezing and Food Safety, USDA FSIS
